#  MediVault – AI-Based Medical Record Management Platform

MediVault is a web-based **Medical Record Management System** developed using **Python and Django**. It provides a centralized platform for managing patients, doctors, hospitals, departments, appointments, and medical reports.

The system is designed to make medical record management easier, more organized, and accessible while providing **AI-assisted medical report analysis

#  Project Overview
Managing medical records manually can be time-consuming and difficult. Patients may have reports from different laboratories and hospitals, while doctors need an organized way to access and review these records.

MediVault provides a digital platform where patient information and medical reports can be managed in one place.
The system supports workflows such as:
- Hospital selection
- Department selection
- Doctor selection
- Patient registration
- Automatic UHID generation
- Appointment management
- Medical report upload
- Medical report viewing
- AI-assisted report analysis
- Doctor remarks
- Role-based authentication
- Email notifications

The main goal of the project is to provide a simple and centralized solution for **digital medical record management

# Objectives
The main objectives of MediVault are:
1. To digitize patient medical records.
2. To provide a centralized medical record management platform.
3. To generate a unique UHID for every patient.
4. To allow patients to register with their selected doctor.
5. To manage doctor and patient information efficiently.
6. To allow medical reports to be uploaded and stored digitally.
7. To provide AI-assisted analysis of uploaded reports.
8. To allow doctors to view patient reports.
9. To allow doctors to add remarks to medical reports.
10. To reduce manual paperwork and improve accessibility of medical information.
11. To provide a structured and user-friendly healthcare workflow.

# AI-Assisted Report Analysis
MediVault includes AI-assisted functionality to analyze uploaded medical reports and provide an understandable interpretation of the available report information.
The AI functionality is intended as an **assistive feature** and is not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis.

# Email Notification
After patient registration, the system can send registration information through email.
The notification can contain information such as:
- Patient name
- Doctor name
- UHID
- Login information
- Registration details

For development, Django's console email backend can be used to display emails in the terminal.
