About MediVault

MediVault is a secure digital medical record management system designed to simplify healthcare data access for patients, doctors, and hospitals. It allows centralized storage of medical reports, quick doctor consultations, and AI-powered report categorization.

Patients can easily access their medical history anytime, while doctors can analyze reports quickly for better diagnosis and treatment.
